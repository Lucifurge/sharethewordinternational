WHO WE ARE

A church for the Word and for the people.

We believe faith is meant to shape the way we worship, love our families, care for our neighbors, and serve the world around us.

Share the Word Apostolic Ministries International exists to make room for people to know God through Scripture, grow through discipleship, and live out the love of Jesus in practical ways. Whether you are exploring faith or have walked with Christ for years, there is room to grow together.
